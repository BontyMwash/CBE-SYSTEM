One Platform. Complete Assessment Management.

BCBE Analytics (B~CBE) brings every part of CBE assessment together.

  • Learners — Full learner register, per class and stream.
  • Teachers — Role-scoped logins for every teacher.
  • Classes & Streams — Grades, streams and CBC sections.
  • Subjects — A shared subject list across the school.
  • Assessments — Every sitting, by type, term and year.
  • Marks Entry — Fast entry with instant % feedback.
  • Performance Analysis — Trends by learner, subject and class.
  • Reports — Auto-generated, printable and exportable.
  • Report Cards — Position, trend chart and comments.
  • Broadsheets — A whole class, every subject, one ledger.
  • SMS Results — Send results straight to parents.
  • School Settings — Grading bands, exam types and branding.
